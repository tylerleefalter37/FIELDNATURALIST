Naturalist Field Manual

1. Combing the Land

Select an ecological zone in [ Comb ] and choose observation actions (Scan Float, Inspect Cutbanks, Sift Gravel, Glass Talus). Finds range from Common Float (Tier 1) to multi-million dollar Tier 5 Crown Jewels.

2. Curatorial Lab

In [ Lab ], grade specimens under stereomicroscopy (up to 2.50x multiplier) and certify them with USGS spectrometry (+40% value boost, doubles auction bidding war odds).

3. 15-Second Auctions

In [ Auction ], global institutions bid autonomously. If a Curatorial Clash occurs, bids spike dramatically. Use [ Hammer Down Early ] or [ Spur the Floor ] to steer the rostrum.

4. Endowments & Atlas

Use earnings to fund 5 tiers of park conservancy projects in [ Endow ]. Hop between all 63 parks in [ Atlas ]—buying the bush plane makes Far North flights permanently free.

5. Leave No Trace & Legal Notice

REAL-WORLD RULE: In actual National Parks, taking rocks, fossils, plants, or artifacts is a federal crime under the Antiquities Act and ARPA. Always practice Leave No Trace.
