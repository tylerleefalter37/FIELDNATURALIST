Naturalist Field Manual

1. Combing the Land

Select an ecological zone in [ Comb ] and choose observation actions (Scan Float, Inspect Cutbanks, Sift Gravel, Glass Talus). Finds range from Common Float (Tier 1) to multi-million dollar Tier 5 Crown Jewels.

2. Curatorial Lab

In [ Lab ], grade specimens under stereomicroscopy (up to 2.50x multiplier) and certify them with USGS spectrometry (+40% value boost, doubles auction bidding war odds).

3. 15-Second Auctions

In [ Auction ], global institutions bid autonomously without self-bidding. If a Curatorial Clash occurs, bids spike dramatically. Use [ Hammer Down Early ] or [ Spur the Floor ] to steer the rostrum.

4. Endowments & Atlas

Use earnings to fund 5 tiers of park conservancy projects in [ Endow ]. Hop between all 63 parks in [ Atlas ]—buying the bush plane makes Far North flights permanently free.

5. Leave No Trace & Institutional Disclaimer

LEAVE NO TRACE: In real National Parks, removing any rock, fossil, mineral, plant, animal trace, or historical artifact is strictly illegal under the Antiquities Act of 1906 and ARPA 1979. Always take only pictures, leave only footprints, and leave historical discoveries in place for archaeologists.
